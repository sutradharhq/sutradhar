# Rules with no case, each with its reason

A rule lands here when no twin pair can measure it: it needs a running
system, a human, time, or production. Anything else gets a case, or a
banked reason in `uncovered.json`. An exclusion without a reason is
refused - a row that cannot say why is an ignore-list row.

- 6.2: restore needs real data
- 6.4: needs a live hypothesis
- 6.5: needs time
- 7.1: needs multiple live sessions
- 7.3: needs multiple live sessions
